      25              : ## 🚀 The Premise
      26              : You control a maintenance drone trapped inside an abandoned research facility. The facility's AI is malfunctioning, and sectors are locked behind **logic-based energy grids**. Your unique ability? **To record and replay your own existence.**
      27              : 
      28              : ### 🛠️ Core Mechanic: The Echo Replication
      29              : *   **🔵 Record:** Press `[Space]` to initialize a 5-second temporal window. All movements and interactions are cached.
      30              : *   **🟣 Replay:** Upon completion, a **Temporal Echo** is spawned—a precise replica of your past self.
      31              : *   **⚡ Simultaneity:** Leverage Echoes to stand on multiple pressure plates, block lethal lasers, and sync complex system activations.

      35              : ## 🏛️ System Architecture
      36              : EchoGrid is built with **S.O.L.I.D.** principles at its core, ensuring a modular, testable, and scalable framework for complex puzzle mechanics.
      37              : 
      38              : *   **`S`ingle Responsibility:** Dedicated modules for Input, Recording, and Interaction.
      39              : *   **`O`pen/Closed:** The `IInteractable` system allows adding new puzzle elements without modifying core player logic.
      40              : *   **`L`iskov Substitution:** Unified interaction protocol for all grid elements (Switches, Plates, Terminals).
      41              : *   **`I`nterface Segregation:** Lean interfaces like `IInteractable` keep the system decoupled and clean.
      42              : *   **`D`ependency Inversion:** A central `EventBus` manages system communication, preventing spaghetti dependencies.
